## What this does and does not establish

**Establishes:** recognition accuracy on this user's accent, how much of their natural speech is romanised rather than Devanagari (which decides how often the slower LLM route is used), and real per-stage latency.

**Does not establish:** whether the synthesised voice *sounds like them*. That is NFR-7 and it requires their own judgement after enrolling a voice profile — run `vaani enroll record`, then `vaani run`, and listen.
